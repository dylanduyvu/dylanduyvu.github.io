# Nebius $775M contract-backed GPU financing, 2026-07-17

## Context

Nebius announced its first senior secured debt facility on 2026-07-17.

Primary release: https://nebius.com/newsroom/nebius-raises-775-million-in-first-secured-debt-financing-to-accelerate-global-buildout

## Disclosed Terms

- Approximately $775M senior secured facility.
- Backed by deployed GPU infrastructure and contracted cash flows from an agreement with an investment-grade customer.
- Matures 2030-10-31.
- Priced at SOFR + 2.50%.
- The customer contract was already in its servicing phase.
- Nebius said the facility and cash flows under the customer agreement together cover more than 100% of the capital expenditure required for the underlying GPU infrastructure.
- The transaction was significantly oversubscribed and led by MUFG, with a broad bank syndicate.
- Nebius described the structure as repeatable across more than $40B of additional contracted revenue from investment-grade customers, naming Microsoft and Meta among those customers.

## Reading

The deal is asset-level and secured by deployed GPUs, but the disclosed repayment support remains an investment-grade customer contract. The GPUs provide collateral and an operating asset. Contracted cash flow makes the facility bankable at an attractive spread.

The use of proceeds does not change that distinction. Nebius can recycle capital from the operational asset into new capacity for a broader customer base, while the $775M facility still leans on the existing customer agreement.

This is fresh evidence for [[gpu-earning-power-is-macro-level-offtake|GPU earning power is macro-level offtake]] and the draft [[the-gpu-backed-credit-market-does-not-exist-yet|GPU Finance Has a Second-Customer Problem]].

## Limits

- The customer is not named in the release.
- The full loan documents and covenant package were not reviewed.
- The release does not decompose how much value lenders assigned to the contract, deployed infrastructure, Nebius's operating history, or other structural protections.
- A secured asset-level facility can still correctly be called GPU-backed even when contracted revenue is its main repayment source.
