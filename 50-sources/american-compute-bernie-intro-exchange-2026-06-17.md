## Bernie's intro policy, mapped

- PEERS AND FELLOW BUILDERS: intros freely, same-day (Barkr, Ornn, both adjacent RVI builders).
- HIS CUSTOMER BASE (lessors, and by the June 16 call's ICP framing, lenders as RVI buyers): DECLINED, with a self-serve workaround offered. The relationship capital is his sales pipeline.
- WEAK TIES: named honestly as weak (SF Compute, Hyperbolic: "not very close").

Consequence, applied 2026-07-14: no intro asks toward his customer side, ever. He already declined once with grace; a second ask makes him repeat himself. Keep-warm replies only. He remains a candidate seat for the later insurability question on the artifact, which is a question FOR him, not a spend OF him.
