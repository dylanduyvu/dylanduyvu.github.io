## Source Claims

- OpenRouter receives about 40 provider applications each day.
- OpenRouter accepts about one provider each day.

## Evidence Limits

- These are in-person event remarks reported by Dylan. They are not a published OpenRouter process document.

## Reasonable Interpretation

A provider can prepare clear information about its legal business, ownership, right to serve each model, handling of customer data, compliance checks, and contract terms. This is preparation guidance, not OpenRouter's exact checklist.
