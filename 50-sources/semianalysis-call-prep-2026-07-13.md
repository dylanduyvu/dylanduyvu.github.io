## Goals, ranked

1. LENDER GROUND TRUTH FROM THE DILIGENCE SEAT. They know what lenders actually commission, which no lender email can tell me at scale. What does an engagement look like, what triggers it, where does it stop?
2. WORK-PRODUCT PORTABILITY, the wedge question asked about their own product. When they produce diligence for one lender, does that work ever get reused by another lender on the same operator, or is it rebuilt per client? Their answer is the artisanal version of the portability thesis, priced and lived.
3. CLUSTERMAX MECHANICS AND EFFECT. Who pays, is operator participation consented, what is the refresh cadence, and the money question: do lenders actually price off the tiers? Any deal where a tier changed terms?
4. SEGMENT RATIO FROM THEIR CHAIR. Of the operators they evaluate or rate, how many have real prior builds that lenders simply cannot read, versus true first-timers? They see more operators than any lender.
5. THE OCCUPANCY PROBE, DE-PRIMED. Where does the practice stop: is everything pre-close, or does anyone commission post-close verification (did the cluster go live, did it hold)? Asked as curiosity about their business, not as a product probe.
6. RELATIONSHIP. Win condition: they leave thinking this person has the boutique-tier lender evidence we do not have, and writes carefully about our work. Becoming a source and peer to the market's information hub is worth more than anything else on this page.
