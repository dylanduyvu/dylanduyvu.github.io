## Durable Delta

Dylan selected a new load-bearing-hypothesis deliverable for Tuesday, August 25, 2026. The output is a plan for the smallest viable inference service and its first five customers. It must connect the Wafer case, minimum infrastructure, AMD rental supply, model selection, subsidy exposure, break-even utilization, and customer acquisition.

This is a decision project, not only a research list. The final plan must select one customer, workload, and model combination, or it must state that no current combination clears the test. It must also state the maximum test loss and the conditions that stop the launch.

## Evidence Boundaries

- The public record does not show a complete account of Wafer's actions before its OpenRouter listing. The plan must separate the known timeline from reasonable inference and private questions.
- A model with demand and few providers is only a candidate. Hardware fit, service performance, operating cost, and customer need require separate checks.
- A free tester, a gateway listing, or routed gateway traffic is not automatically a customer. The project uses a paid direct account or a paid pilot as the working customer definition.
- The public vault must not contain private customer data, credentials, or confidential commercial terms.
