# GPU lender wave two: expansion draft packet

Purpose: expand the cleaned wave-two cohort to an experiment-sized group without filling it with generic inboxes or unsupported guesses. Every recipient below is tied to a named GPU, AI data-center, or HPC financing.

No Gmail drafts have been created from this packet yet.

## Result

- Expansion researched: 22 people across 19 firms.
- Ready: 19 people. Eight addresses are exact-public and eleven are strong employee-format matches.
- Hold: 3 people whose role is excellent but whose candidate address has a meaningful collision or format risk.
- Combined with the 16 ready contacts in [[gpu-lender-wave-two-cleaned-draft-packet-2026-07-13]], the sendable cohort is 35 people. The three holds take the full researched cohort to 38.
- The packet intentionally retains a few same-firm contacts. Dylan has said duplicates are acceptable, and each person has a different seat or deal-specific question.

## Confidence labels

- `ready, exact-public`: the person's address appears verbatim in a public source.
- `ready, strong-pattern`: the role and deal are verified, and at least two named employee addresses support the mailbox format.
- `hold`: the person is relevant, but the inferred mailbox has enough uncertainty that it should not be staged yet.

## Send rules

1. Translate only the 19 ready entries into Gmail unless Dylan explicitly includes the hold cohort.
2. Search Gmail for an existing thread with the recipient before creating a new message.
3. Keep each message plain text. No links, attachments, meeting ask, or em dash.
4. Each email has one context paragraph and one question.
5. Do not imply personal deal involvement when only the firm is named. Those messages use firm-level language.
6. For firms with two contacts, keep both drafts but surface the duplication before sending.
