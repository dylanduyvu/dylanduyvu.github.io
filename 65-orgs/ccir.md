# CCIR

## Context

Compute Credit Index Research is building a public reference-data layer for GPU compute and GPU-backed credit. Its site publishes daily rental rates, term curves, credit-facility records, secondary-market observations, utilization data, research, and worked examples for using its rates in loan documents. CCIR says the Delaware company was formed on 2026-03-11.

## Why It Matters

CCIR is both a valuable research source and a competitor to several ideas in the GPU-finance problem portfolio. It already occupies much of the public-pricing, public-facility-data, simple residual-data, and market-monitoring surface.

The remaining openings are more demanding:

- institutional bulk sale and recovery data;
- live bookable inventory and transaction workflow;
- realized fleet utilization;
- borrower or lender operating software;
- underwriting decisions and private terms;
- products that transfer risk rather than only describe it.

## Current Read

- **Strongest data:** public loan terms, rating actions, posted rental panels, committed-term curves, and a clearly separated retail sold-listing record.
- **Most important limitation:** several series are new or thin, and retail sale evidence is not a bulk liquidation tape.
- **Most relevant strategic signal:** the generic data layer is filling faster than the utilization, workflow, and risk-transfer layers.
- **Adoption question:** CCIR publishes hypothetical covenant language, but no executed loan has yet been found citing its rates.
