# FarmGPU

## Context

GPU infrastructure operator used by [[silicon-network|Silicon Network]] for Cluster 00 and Cluster 01 deployments. Silicon pool capital funds hardware that FarmGPU deploys and operates.

## Relationship With Silicon

McDavid Stoddard said Silicon has only used FarmGPU and has not had to diligence another operator. Asked how Silicon originally got comfortable with FarmGPU, he said "JM is an advisor to us," then clarified that JM is FarmGPU's CEO. JM's full name was not established in the exchange.

McDavid added that "a lot of this portion of the industry is very relationship driven." The direct observation is that FarmGPU's CEO already advised Silicon, providing a relationship-based trust path before capital was deployed. This is not evidence of a repeatable third-party verification process.

## Strategic Read

- FarmGPU is evidence that trusted relationships can substitute for portable operator history at the tokenized GPU-capital tier.
- The case does not validate willingness to pay for operator verification because Silicon avoided the unknown-operator decision.
- Reopen the verification question only if Silicon evaluates an operator beyond FarmGPU or FarmGPU seeks financing from a new capital provider that cannot rely on the advisor relationship.
