# Venice AI

## Context

Venice AI (founder Erik Voorhees, ex-ShapeShift) is a privacy-first, uncensored inference platform: zero-retention proxy architecture, 200+ open-source and frontier models, crypto-native (VVV token; MOR holders get free Pro). Relevant to this vault as a worked example of demand-tracing (a funded inference platform whose spend locates downstream compute revenue) and as a coming lease-to-own GPU financing event.

## Known Facts / Signals

- 2026-07-01: raised $65M Series A at $1B valuation (Dragonfly-led; Coinbase Ventures, North Island Ventures) - first external round. ~$70M annualized revenue, reportedly profitable; 3M+ users, ~1.7M API calls/day.
- GPU supply today: a decentralized/DePIN pool - Akash, Hyperbolic, Prime Intellect, Morpheus - plus routed closed-model APIs for frontier models. Morpheus is the ideologically central node (Voorhees launch statements; MOR perks) but not the sole supplier; the viral "it all traces to Morpheus" answer overclaims.
- Stated use of raise: buy GPUs and build own data centers to stop leasing and expand gross margins (lease-to-own migration; puts the obsolescence curve on Venice's balance sheet, and disintermediates its current DePIN suppliers).
- March 2026: added TEE and E2EE verifiable-encryption inference modes (NEAR AI Cloud, Phala).

## Open Questions

- Who finances the fleet purchase when Venice verticalizes, and is it shaped like a USD.AI GPU-backed deal?
- Are its DePIN suppliers financeable operators, or does distributed/pseudonymous hardware break perfectable-title underwriting?
