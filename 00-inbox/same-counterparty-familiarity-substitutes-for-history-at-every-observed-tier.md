# Same-counterparty familiarity is the working substitute for verifiable history at every tier observed so far

STATUS: hunch, possible duplicate. The observation: three different tiers named the same substitute in one day, none of them prompted. A participant in Apollo and Blackstone club deals relies on the lead's reputation and the lead's pack (Phil, 2026-07-13). Venture debt re-lends to its own book (the track-record post, weakness three). If the same substitute operates at every tier, then the market-wide answer to missing verifiable history is not better diligence, it is staying with who you already know, which is the thesis's demand mechanism showing up as observed behavior rather than argued structure.
