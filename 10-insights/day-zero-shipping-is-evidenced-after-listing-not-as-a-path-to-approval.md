# Day-0 shipping is evidenced after listing, not as a path to approval

## Claim

The current provider case studies support day-0 model shipping as a post-listing growth tactic. They do not show that day-0 shipping caused a provider to get listed on OpenRouter.

“Ship a gap-filling model, then apply” remains a reasonable hypothesis because OpenRouter states that it prioritizes providers that fill network gaps. It is not a case-proven listing playbook.

## Why It Matters

The distinction separates documented behavior from causal inference. Parasail, Inceptron, Baseten, Chutes, and SiliconFlow show that fast model launches can win traffic, price position, and attention after a provider has access to a gateway. Their public accounts do not show the application, named contact, review timeline, approval criteria, or event that caused acceptance.

Using those companies as evidence for a pre-listing tactic would give a hypothesis the status of a result.

## Evidence

- **Direct report evidence:** The case studies document day-0 launches after providers were already live or had launch-partner access.
- **Direct counterevidence inside the same report:** The Inceptron section says its application channel, contact, onboarding time, and listing sequence were not public. The caveats say approval mechanics are almost never published.
- **Official source claim:** OpenRouter says it gives priority to providers that fill gaps in its network. This supports the gap-filling hypothesis but does not establish which provider action causes approval.
- **Belief correction:** Dylan's 2026-08-18 audit identified the unsupported weld between the official priority statement and the post-listing launch posts.

## Implications

- Relabel the day-0 material as a guide to growth after listing.
- Describe “ship a gap-filling model, then apply” as a testable hypothesis.
- Do not cite post-listing traffic results as evidence of the listing decision.
- If the hypothesis is tested, record the application channel, contacts, elapsed time, review requests, technical tests, decision, and later traffic separately.

## Counterpoints / Uncertainty

- A live gap-filling endpoint can still improve an application. The current public record does not establish whether it does.
- Private provider conversations may contain the missing causal evidence.
- OpenRouter's process and priorities can change.
