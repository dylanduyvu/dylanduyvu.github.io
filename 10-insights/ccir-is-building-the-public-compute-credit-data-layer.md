# CCIR is building the public compute-credit data layer

## Claim

The public GPU-finance data layer is no longer empty. CCIR already publishes rental reference rates, committed-term curves, credit-facility records, secondary-market observations, realized-versus-posted revenue data, utilization work, an explorer, and examples of how its rates could enter loan documents.

A generic public GPU price database, simple facility tracker, basic rental aggregator, or public residual index is therefore not clean whitespace.

## Why It Matters

A new product now needs proprietary value beyond those public tables. The remaining openings are more likely to sit in:

- institutional dealer, lessor, auction, and foreclosure records;
- live bookable inventory and transaction workflow;
- realized fleet utilization and customer conversion;
- private underwriting decisions and loan terms;
- borrower or lender operating software;
- products that absorb or transfer risk.

The strongest adjacent direction in the current vault is borrower-side finance workflow. CCIR supplies public inputs, but an operator still has to maintain asset schedules, map contracts to debt, calculate coverage and reserves, satisfy draw conditions, report to lenders, and refinance.

## Counterpoints / Uncertainty

- CCIR is new, and several series are still thin.
- Publishing data is not the same as winning lender adoption or a durable commercial budget.
- Live inventory, buyer routing, and transaction execution may still be unsolved even if static public comparison is crowded.
